────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  POST 4/5 — ONNX – Interoperability Standard
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  🔥 HOOK
  Why are top AI teams swapping models between frameworks without a hitch? 🔄

  📌 SUMMARY
  Open format that lets you move models from PyTorch ↔ TensorFlow ↔ Edge devices
  Supports over 100 operators and hardware accelerators
  Reduces deployment friction and speeds up time‑to‑market

  🔗 LINK
  https://github.com/onnx/onnx

  ✍️  CAPTION
  🔗 **One model, many worlds**
  ONNX (Open Neural Network Exchange) is the universal passport for AI models. Train in PyTorch, export to ONNX, and run it on TensorRT, CoreML, or even a microcontroller.
  
  🛠️ **Technical perks**
  Standardized graph representation, versioned opsets, and a thriving ecosystem of converters make migration painless.
  
  🚀 **Boost your workflow**
  No more re‑writing code for each platform. Deploy faster, iterate smarter, and keep your model stack future‑proof.
  
  💬 Drop a comment if you’ve ever struggled with model conversion—ONNX might be the answer you’ve been waiting for!
