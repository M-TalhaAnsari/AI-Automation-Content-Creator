  ✍️  CAPTION
  Hey tech enthusiasts! 👋 Are you ready to take your Docker skills to the next level? 🚀 Let's break down the Docker container lifecycle. 
             
             The Docker container lifecycle is a series of stages that a container goes through, from creation to deletion. It's essential to understand these stages to manage containers effectively. 
             
             Here's a brief overview of the 5 stages: 
             1. **Created**: The container is created, but it's not running yet. 
             2. **Running**: The container is up and running, and you can execute commands inside it. 
             3. **Paused**: The container is paused, and all processes are suspended. 
             4. **Stopped**: The container is stopped, and all processes are terminated. 
             5. **Deleted**: The container is deleted, and all resources are released. 
             
             Understanding the Docker container lifecycle will help you troubleshoot issues, optimize resource usage, and improve your overall Docker experience. So, make sure to master this concept to become a Docker pro! 💻
