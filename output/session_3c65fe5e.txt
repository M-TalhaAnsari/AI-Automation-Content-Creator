  POST 5/5 — Securing Your Slack Client Communications
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  🔥 HOOK
  🔐 Your Slack tokens are the keys—protect them like gold!

  📌 SUMMARY
  Token storage best practices
  Encrypted transport (TLS)
  Pitfall: hard‑coding in scripts

  ✍️  CAPTION
  Every Slack client—CLI, SDK, or third‑party app—relies on an OAuth token to authenticate API calls. Store tokens in environment variables or secret managers, never in plain‑text scripts. All Slack traffic is forced over TLS, ensuring encryption in transit, but the endpoint is only as secure as the token you present. A frequent security lapse is hard‑coding the token into a repo; once exposed, attackers can hijack your workspace. 🛡️ Save this guide to keep your Slack integrations airtight and drop a comment with your security checklist!
