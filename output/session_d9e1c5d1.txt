  POST 5/5 — Predictive Maintenance Dashboard for Wind Farms
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  🔥 HOOK
  🌬️ Keep turbines humming and cut downtime by 40% with AI!

  📌 SUMMARY
  Time‑series feature extraction with tsfresh
  LightGBM regression for RUL prediction
  Docker‑compose deployment on on‑prem edge servers

  🔗 LINK
  https://magneticmarketing.com/blog/20-types-of-business-industries-the-complete-list

  ✍️  CAPTION
  **Project Overview**
  A sleek dashboard that forecasts the Remaining Useful Life (RUL) of each turbine blade, enabling maintenance crews to schedule visits before a failure occurs – saving millions in lost production ⚡.
  
  **Tech Stack Breakdown**
  - **tsfresh** automatically extracts hundreds of statistical features from vibration & temperature sensors.
  - **LightGBM** regression model predicts RUL with <5% error.
  - **InfluxDB** stores high‑frequency sensor streams.
  - **Grafana** visualises live health scores and alerts.
  - **Docker‑compose** bundles the pipeline for edge deployment at the wind farm site.
  
  **Core System Architecture**
  1️⃣ Sensors stream data to InfluxDB via MQTT.
  2️⃣ Batch job runs tsfresh nightly, feeding features to LightGBM.
  3️⃣ Model outputs RUL predictions stored back in InfluxDB.
  4️⃣ Grafana panels display degradation curves and trigger email/SMS alerts when thresholds are crossed.
